# Opportunity Shaping Skill (PM-07)

## Purpose
Transforms market signals and user friction into rigorous, peer-reviewed Opportunity Development Packs (`OPP-XXXX`).

## Opportunity Framework
Every opportunity pack must define:
1. **Opportunity ID & Title:** `OPP-XXXX` format.
2. **Problem Statement:** Grounded in user friction, market vacuum, or technical bottleneck.
3. **Evidence Base:** Direct empirical citations from user logs, bug tickets, or competitor benchmarks.
4. **Counter-Evidence & Risks:** Concrete reasons NOT to build it (e.g. blast radius, complexity, cognitive overload).
5. **Alternatives Considered:** Lightweight or zero-code alternatives evaluated.
6. **Technical Architecture & Migration Plan:** Schema changes, client/server boundaries, and offline fallback.
7. **Success Metrics:** Quantifiable target outcomes (e.g. sync conflict rate < 0.01%).
8. **Owner Decision Gate:** Explicit prompt for Ahmad to approve, reject, or revise.
