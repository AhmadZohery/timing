# Competitor Research Skill (PM-02 / PM-07)

## Purpose
Monitors competitor feature releases, pricing changes, technical limitations, and user grievances to position Midmar LifeOS as the definitive sovereign life operating system.

## Benchmark Categories
- **Productivity & Time:** TickTick, Todoist, Notion, Forest, Habitica, Sunsama.
- **Spiritual & Islamic:** Muslim Pro, Tarteel AI, Pillars, Quran.com, Golden Quran.
- **Language Learning:** Duolingo, Anki, Memrise, Pimsleur.

## Analysis Vector
1. **Offline & Privacy Sovereignty:** Does the competitor work without internet? Do they collect user telemetry?
2. **Integration Depth:** Do they unite prayer rhythms with cognitive work blocks or are they siloed?
3. **UX Friction:** Ad bloat, subscription walls, load latencies, and gesture clumsiness.
