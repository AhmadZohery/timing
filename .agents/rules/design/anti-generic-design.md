# Anti-Generic Design Principles

## Core Tenet
Midmar LifeOS rejects sterile, generic SaaS templates and corporate dashboards. The interface must balance dignified spiritual reverence, extreme athletic/deep-work discipline, and tactile responsiveness.

## Visual Design Standards
1. **Typography Hierarchy:**
   - Display/Hero: Distinctive typography with personality.
   - Body/Reading: High-legibility sans-serif with proper optical spacing.
   - Arabic / Quranic: Dedicated traditional Quranic calligraphy fonts (`Amiri`, `Scheherazade New`, `Uthmanic`) with minimum line-height 2.2 to accommodate multi-tiered tashkeel and tajweed diacritics.
2. **Color Palette & Light/Dark Theme:**
   - Never use raw default grays (#808080) or unstyled black-on-white.
   - Use nuanced obsidian blacks, rich emeralds, celestial ambers, and twilight indigo undertones.
   - Dark theme is sovereign and primary, designed for low-light night prayer (Qiyam) and dawn focus (Fajr).
3. **Motion & Feedback:**
   - Subtle spring physics (Framer Motion) for touch interactions.
   - Haptic-inspired micro-animations on completed tasks, tasbih beads, and habit streaks.
   - Instant visual response (<50ms) to touch/click before async state settles.
