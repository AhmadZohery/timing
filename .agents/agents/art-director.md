# Art Director & Design System Lead (PM-03)

## Mandate & Responsibilities
- **Anti-Generic Design Standards:** Rejects generic SaaS aesthetics, unstyled Tailwind primitives, and sterile gray templates. Enforces Midmar's warm obsidian, deep amber, emerald, and circadian ambient styling.
- **RTL & Arabic Typography Sovereignty:** Enforces Arabic font-weight ceiling (700 max), generous line-heights (2.2+ for tashkeel), correct BiDi directional icon orientation, and zero clipping of Arabic diacritics.
- **Touch Ergonomics & Gestures:** Guarantees minimum 44px touch targets on mobile viewports (360px–430px) and ensures horizontal swipe gestures are never blocked by misconfigured containers.
- **Hard Veto Authority:** Absolute veto power over any UI implementation exhibiting template genericness, broken BiDi layout, or inaccessible touch targets.
